**Challenges & Capabilities**

## **Electrifying the Airports Is the New Norm.**

**Incapable chargers have made horrifying experiences on the ramp yet, so staff fear chargers. But not anymore.**

**1\. Cables always on the ground, at risk of damage?**  
A retractor pulls the cables up after every charge, keeping them off the ground and away from wheels.

**2\. Never sure which vehicle is fully charged?**  
Cumulus (our live dashboard) shows every vehicle's charge level, with a ready-to-use list for your team.

**3\. Too little time to charge between flights?**  
Dual 20 kW ports charge two vehicles at once, so even short stops between flights become charging time.

**4\. Can one small fault stop the whole charger?**  
The dual charging ports work independently. If one has a fault, the other keeps charging as normal.

**5\. Finding out about a fault too late?**  
Cumulus (our live dashboard) sends an alert the moment a fault appears, naming the charger and the problem.

**6\. Could a charger repair take many days?**  
The screen shows the exact fault, and the power modules slide out like drawers for quick replacement.

**7\. Will your power supply handle more chargers?**  
The charger can run on spare power from your passenger bridges, so adding chargers need not mean adding supply.

**8\. Different vehicles, each needing a different charger?**  
A single charger for all your vehicles (48 to 1000 volts), charging buses, trucks, passenger EVs, high-voltage ground equipment, and forklifts.

**9\. Different battery types, at risk of wrong charging?**  
CellTrac (a small tracker on each battery) tells the charger the battery type, so lead-acid and lithium each charge correctly.

**10\. Batteries at risk of wearing out early?**  
Every charge adjusts to the battery's temperature, and CellTrac (a small tracker on each battery) records its health over time.

**11\. Never sure where each vehicle is?**  
CellTrac (a small tracker on each battery) follows every vehicle by GPS and shows them all on one live map.

**12\. Shared chargers, but who pays how much?**  
Cumulus (our live dashboard) records each company's charging time and energy used, then prepares the bill automatically.

**13\. Vehicles parked far from any power point?**  
Mobilus (a battery charger on wheels) goes to where vehicles are parked, with six ports and no power point needed there.
