## **Some Questions You May Have**

**Here's generally what most people ask frequently before deploying Altus II.**

&nbsp;

### Why Is Altus II Right Fit For Us?

It charges GSE and small EVs across Lead Acid, Lithium, or EV batteries, with dual ports plus an optional 3rd port for passenger EVs.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

### What Makes It Different From Other Chargers?

Cables retract automatically instead of sitting exposed on the ramp, a 7-inch touchscreen shows live status, and cloud dashboards give visibility most chargers don't offer.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

### What Charging Connectors Does Altus II Support?

Altus II supports Anderson, Euro 320, REMA 320, and BIW connectors as standard, with an optional J1772 connector added for Level 2 passenger EV charging.&nbsp;

### Is Altus II Certified For Safety Standards?

Yes. Altus II is UL1564 certified, the safety standard built specifically for electric industrial vehicle chargers, including the equipment used across airport ground operations.&nbsp;

### Has Altus II Been Deployed Before?&nbsp;

Yes, deployed at airports worldwide. It's pedestal-mounted for flexible ramp placement, no wall space or structural changes required to install it.
