## **A3. Terms Glossed in Plain Language**

| Term | Plain-language gloss |
| :---- | :---- |
| DC fast charging | Charging that feeds direct current straight to the battery, faster than a standard AC charger. |
| Opportunity charging | Short top-up charges during breaks and shift changes instead of one long overnight charge. |
| GSE | Ground support equipment: tugs, belt loaders, pushbacks and other airside vehicles. |
| Silicon Carbide (SiC) | A newer power semiconductor material that loses less energy as heat, allowing smaller chargers. |
| CAN | A standard data link vehicles and batteries use to share status with the charger. |
| AGM | Absorbed glass mat: a sealed type of lead acid battery. |
| CCS1, CCS2, NACS, GB/T | Plug standards used by on-road EVs in North America, Europe and China. |
| SB350, Anderson, Euro, REMA | Industrial plug standards used on forklifts and GSE. |
| UL 1564 / UL 2202 / UL 2231 | Safety standards for industrial battery chargers, EV charging equipment and personnel protection. |
| SCADA / ERP | The control and business software airports and large sites already run. |
| Cumulus | Our cloud fleet dashboard. |
| CellTrac | Our battery monitoring and telematics system. |
| Intelligent power sharing | Directing available electrical capacity to the vehicles that need it. |
