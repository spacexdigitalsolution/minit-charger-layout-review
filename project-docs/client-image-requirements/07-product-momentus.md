## 2. Detailed Creative Shot Descriptions

### Asset 07.1: Hero Distribution Center Workstation Shot

- **Target File Path / Component Reference:** `/assets/homepage/Meet MOMENTUs-02.webp`
- **Recommended Dimensions & Aspect Ratio:** `2560 x 1440 px (16:9)`
- **Asset Type:** On-Location Warehouse Photography
- **File Format:** WebP / JPG
- **Production Priority:** P0 (Launch Critical)

#### Creative Shot Description & Framing Directive:
> Environmental shot of Momentus mounted to a warehouse structural column directly adjacent to a fast-paced pallet staging zone. Forklift driver plugged in during break. High-bay industrial warehouse environment with clean yellow lane striping and high-density racking.

#### Photographer / 3D Artist Instructions:
* Capture in RAW/DNG format at maximum resolution (minimum 24 megapixels).
* Avoid distracting clutter in the background; ensure equipment operators wear appropriate PPE (safety vests, eye protection, ear defenders).
* Color grade for industrial realism with saturated safety accents (green LED indicators, yellow safety striping, safety-orange equipment).
