- **Recommended Dimensions & Aspect Ratio:** `2560 x 1440 px (16:9)`
- **Asset Type:** On-Location Commercial Photography
- **File Format:** WebP / JPG
- **Production Priority:** P0 (Launch Critical)

#### Creative Shot Description & Framing Directive:
> Photograph of Pharus dual-port Level 2 chargers installed in a modern airport administration or corporate campus parking plaza. Electric employee sedans and security SUVs plugged in. Clean painted stalls, clear signage, daytime natural lighting.

#### Photographer / 3D Artist Instructions:
