- **Recommended Dimensions & Aspect Ratio:** `2560 x 1440 px (16:9)`
- **Asset Type:** On-Location Vehicle Photography
- **File Format:** WebP / JPG
- **Production Priority:** P0 (Launch Critical)

#### Creative Shot Description & Framing Directive:
> Close-up environmental photograph of Durus mounted directly onto the rugged steel frame of an electric campus utility vehicle or burden carrier. Shows sealed aluminum enclosure resisting vibration, dust, and outdoor conditions.
