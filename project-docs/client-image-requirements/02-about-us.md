- **Asset Type:** On-Location Corporate Photography
- **File Format:** WebP / JPG
- **Production Priority:** P0 (Launch Critical)

#### Creative Shot Description & Framing Directive:
> Wide environmental photograph of Minit Charger's Chandler, Arizona engineering and assembly facility. Shows state-of-the-art testing bays, technicians in branded gear assembling power cabinets, and electrical test benches with oscilloscopes and calibration meters.
