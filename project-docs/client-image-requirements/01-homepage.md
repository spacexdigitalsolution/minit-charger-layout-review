## 2. Detailed Creative Shot Descriptions

### Asset 01.1: Hero Carousel - Slide 1 (Aviation GSE)

- **Target File Path / Component Reference:** `/assets/homepage/Outdoor eGSE & yards-01.webp`
- **Recommended Dimensions & Aspect Ratio:** `2560 x 1440 px (16:9 Widescreen)`
- **Asset Type:** On-Location Industrial Photography
- **File Format:** WebP / 24-bit JPG (Q90+)
- **Production Priority:** P0 (Launch Critical)

#### Creative Shot Description & Framing Directive:
> Wide-angle, cinematic photograph of an Altus II fast-charging station installed on an active airport tarmac/apron at dawn or golden hour. An electric baggage tug or belt loader is plugged in with its charging status LED illuminated. A commercial passenger aircraft (e.g. Boeing 737 / Airbus A320) is visible in the background ramp area. Crisp, industrial lighting with high contrast and morning sky glow.

#### Photographer / 3D Artist Instructions:
* Capture in RAW/DNG format at maximum resolution (minimum 24 megapixels).
* Avoid distracting clutter in the background; ensure equipment operators wear appropriate PPE (safety vests, eye protection, ear defenders).
* Color grade for industrial realism with saturated safety accents (green LED indicators, yellow safety striping, safety-orange equipment).
