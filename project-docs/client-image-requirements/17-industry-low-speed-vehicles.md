### Asset 17.1: Hero Campus / Resort LSV Staging Area

- **Target File Path / Component Reference:** `/assets/homepage/Engineered for LSEVs.webp`
- **Recommended Dimensions & Aspect Ratio:** `2560 x 1440 px (16:9)`
- **Asset Type:** Commercial Scenic Photography
- **File Format:** WebP / JPG
- **Production Priority:** P0 (Launch Critical)

#### Creative Shot Description & Framing Directive:
> Scenic wide-angle photograph of an upscale college campus or luxury resort charging staging area. Multiple electric golf carts, campus maintenance trucks, and passenger shuttles plugged in at charging stations. Lush greenery and modern architecture in background.

#### Photographer / 3D Artist Instructions:
* Capture in RAW/DNG format at maximum resolution (minimum 24 megapixels).

* Color grade for industrial realism with saturated safety accents (green LED indicators, yellow safety striping, safety-orange equipment).
