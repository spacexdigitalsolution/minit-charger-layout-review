### Asset 08.1: Hero High-Throughput Fulfillment Center

- **Target File Path / Component Reference:** `/assets/homepage/Indoor material handling-01.webp`
- **Recommended Dimensions & Aspect Ratio:** `2560 x 1440 px (16:9)`
- **Asset Type:** On-Location Industrial Photography
- **File Format:** WebP / JPG
- **Production Priority:** P0 (Launch Critical)

#### Creative Shot Description & Framing Directive:
> Action shot of Maximus dual-port charger situated between two busy warehouse staging aisles. Two electric forklifts are connected simultaneously to dual 20 kW ports, both charging at full rate during shift crossover. Clean, bustling logistics warehouse environment.

#### Photographer / 3D Artist Instructions:

* Color grade for industrial realism with saturated safety accents (green LED indicators, yellow safety striping, safety-orange equipment).
