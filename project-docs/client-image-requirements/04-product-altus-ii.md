### Asset 04.1: Hero Ramp Panoramic Banner

- **Target File Path / Component Reference:** `/assets/Industries/Aviation-GSE/Why Minit Charger/Banner.webp`
- **Recommended Dimensions & Aspect Ratio:** `2560 x 1440 px (16:9)`
- **Asset Type:** On-Location Aviation Photography
- **File Format:** WebP / JPG
- **Production Priority:** P0 (Launch Critical)

#### Creative Shot Description & Framing Directive:
> Cinematic wide photography of Altus II deployed directly on an airport apron beside a passenger terminal gate. In the foreground, an electric baggage tractor is plugged into port 1 while a belt loader is parked in port 2. The overhead retractable cable arm holds heavy DC cords safely above the pavement. Late afternoon airport light with glowing runway runway background.

#### Photographer / 3D Artist Instructions:
* Capture in RAW/DNG format at maximum resolution (minimum 24 megapixels).
* Avoid distracting clutter in the background; ensure equipment operators wear appropriate PPE (safety vests, eye protection, ear defenders).
* Color grade for industrial realism with saturated safety accents (green LED indicators, yellow safety striping, safety-orange equipment).
