Gate 2: Design Theory & Visual Direction

Heavy-duty industrial clarity meets Swiss architectural precision for Minit Charger fleet electrification.

Core Aesthetic Tenets

  • Brutalist Industrial Precision: High-density technical data, sharp border dividers, mono kickers, zero frivolous ornament.
  • Monochrome Palette: Zinc-950 deep contrasts, pure white backgrounds, and electric green (#16a34a) accents.
  • Engineered Typography: Display bold headers paired with geometric sans and monospace telemetry badges.
  • Fluid Motion: Scroll-driven GSAP timelines with strict reduced-motion accessibility overrides.
