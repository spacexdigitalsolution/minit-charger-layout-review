**Salient Features**

## **Not Like the Rest, But Beyond**

### Your Compact Powerhouse

Delivers 40kW of dual-port charging without taking up extra space on your ramp.

### Charges Anything, Anywhere

Dual low-voltage ports for tugs, belt loaders, and baggage tractors on lead-acid or lithium, plus a J1772 port for passenger EVs. Battery settings are adjusted remotely, with no site visit.

### Power On Demand

Each port runs on its own 20 kW. If one has a fault, the other keeps charging as normal.

### Engineered for Zero Damage

The cable retracts automatically, never touching the ground, and detaches for easy swaps.

**FAQs**

## **Some Questions You May Have**

**Here's generally what most people ask frequently before deploying Altus II.**

### Why Is Altus II Right Fit For Us?

It charges ground equipment on lead-acid or lithium batteries through two independent ports, plus a J1772 port for passenger EVs.

### What Makes It Different From Other Chargers?

Cables retract automatically instead of sitting exposed on the ramp, a 7-inch touchscreen shows live status, and cloud dashboards give visibility most chargers don't offer. 

### What Charging Connectors Does Altus II Support?

Altus II supports Anderson, Euro 320, REMA 320, and BIW connectors as standard, with an optional J1772 connector added for Level 2 passenger EV charging. 

### Is Altus II Certified For Safety Standards?

Yes. Altus II is UL1564 certified, the safety standard built specifically for electric industrial vehicle chargers, including the equipment used across airport ground operations. 

### Has Altus II Been Deployed Before? 

Yes, deployed at airports worldwide. It's pedestal-mounted for flexible ramp placement, no wall space or structural changes required to install it.
