**Minit Charger vs. PosiCharge Competitive Positioning**  
**Competitive Overview**

Minit Charger and PosiCharge both provide intelligent fast-charging solutions for electric GSE, industrial vehicles, and fleet applications. Both companies offer adaptive charging, battery monitoring, cloud connectivity, multi-chemistry support, and infrastructure power-management capabilities.  
Minit Charger’s strongest competitive differentiation is its **modern, compact charging architecture and ability to consolidate a broad range of charging applications into fewer charger platforms**. Products such as Momentus and Maximus use Silicon Carbide (SiC) power electronics to provide high output from a compact wall- or pedestal-mounted enclosure, while Magnus extends the Minit platform from traditional low-voltage GSE through high-voltage EV charging.   
PosiCharge has a highly established eGSE product line with significant airport deployment history, scalable multi-vehicle charging systems, mature battery-management tools, and sophisticated power-sharing capabilities. Its DVS/MVS products remain particularly strong for large distributed GSE charging installations.

**Direct Product Comparison Highlights**  
**Maximus vs. PosiCharge DVS400**  
The Maximus and DVS400 provide one of the clearest direct comparisons because both are positioned as approximately **40 kW dual-vehicle charging systems**.

| Specification | Minit Maximus | PosiCharge DVS400 |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Maximum Output Power | 40 kW | 40 kW |
| Charging Ports | Dual | Dual |
| Maximum Efficiency | 94% | 90% |
| Power Technology | SiC | IGBT |
| Published Charger Dimensions | 22 × 20 × 12 in\* | 60 × 32.4 × 21.9 in |
| Published Weight | 60 lb\* | 915 lb |
| Mounting | Wall or pedestal | Floor-standing |
| Battery Voltage Range | 24–100 VDC | 24–96 VDC |
| Battery Communication | CAN and/or Bluetooth | BMID / CAN support |
| Cloud Connectivity | AssetPro / Cumulus | PosiCharge digital ecosystem |

\*The Maximus dimensional and weight values should be confirmed as **charger-enclosure-only values** before being used in public comparative advertising, because other Minit documentation includes pedestal dimensions/weight separately.   
The DVS400 catalog lists 40 kW output, 90% efficiency, a 915 lb weight, and a 60 × 32.4 × 21.9-inch enclosure. 

**Positioning takeaway:** Maximus provides comparable nominal charging power from a substantially more compact architecture while improving published efficiency in this direct configuration.  
---

**Momentus vs. PosiCharge SVS100**  
Momentus is positioned as a compact 20 kW single-port charger with 94% maximum efficiency, wall-mount capability, multiple battery chemistries, removable cable architecture, and AssetPro connectivity.   
The PosiCharge SVS100 is a 10 kW single-port charger using IGBT technology with a published efficiency of 91%, 388 lb weight, and dimensions of approximately 39.4 × 22 × 19.3 inches.   
**Positioning takeaway:** Momentus targets customers who want higher charging capability without moving to a large traditional charger cabinet.  
---

**Magnus vs. PosiCharge High Voltage Power Station**  
Magnus represents the strongest differentiation at the upper end of the Minit portfolio.  
The current Magnus literature positions the system at **100–200 kW and 24–1000 VDC**, supporting both traditional GSE connectors and high-voltage EV connectors including CCS and NACS options.   
PosiCharge's High Voltage Power Station extends its product family into higher-voltage equipment and road EV charging, but the current catalog describes it as a **single-port 20 kW unit when powered from the PosiCharge DC bus or up to 30 kW from AC input**, with a 250–920 VDC output range and CCS1/NACS connectivity. 

**Positioning takeaway:**  
**Magnus consolidates traditional GSE and high-voltage EV charging into one high-power platform rather than requiring separate low- and high-voltage charger architectures.**  
For airports transitioning fleets over many years, this creates a strong **future-proofing** message.
